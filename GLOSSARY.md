# Profile

The GitHub profile of Sang Nguyen: the README, the sidebar and the pinned repos, all told through one theme, 死に戻り (Return by Death): die, come back, try again.

## Language

**Loop**:
One revolution of the Clock: one calendar year of contributions. The current year is the loop in progress.
_Avoid_: Run, cycle, season

**Clock**:
The ring at the top of the README with one tick per day of the past year, around the words 死に戻り.
_Avoid_: Ring, dial, wheel

**City**:
The past year of contributions drawn as 不夜城, the city that never sleeps: one isometric block per day.
_Avoid_: Skyline, contribution graph, heatmap

**Off the clock**:
The anime corner of the README: favorite characters, one line each.
_Avoid_: Anime panel, hobbies

**Attempt**:
One run of Subaru across the City, from the first week to today. Attempts repeat for as long as the README is open.
_Avoid_: Run, loop, try

**Subaru**:
Natsuki Subaru, at his own proportions, running the City's rooftops one week per hop on every Attempt.
_Avoid_: Mascot, chibi, sprite, runner

**Save point**:
The rooftop just before the City's longest stretch of empty weeks, where Subaru returns after he dies there, then tries again.
_Avoid_: Checkpoint, respawn
