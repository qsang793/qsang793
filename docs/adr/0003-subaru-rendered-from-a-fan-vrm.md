# Subaru is rendered from a fan VRM model, and only the renders are kept

Subaru runs the City as a handful of frames rendered in Blender from "Natsuki Subaru" by Madoka-Senpai on VRoid Hub (https://hub.vroid.com/en/characters/2527299861430843430/models/3392138998639283709), posed with Mixamo animations. The model's terms allow modification but not redistribution, so the repo keeps the rendered frames in `assets/subaru/` and the Blender script that makes them, never the `.vrm`; anyone rebuilding the frames downloads the model themselves. Like the portraits in ADR 0002, the character belongs to its rights holders and could draw a takedown, a risk accepted for the profile. The model's terms ask for no credit; the README credits Madoka-Senpai anyway.

## Considered Options

- Downloaded chibi images from the web: none showed one consistent chibi running, jumping and falling, and most were fan art with no one to credit.
- Luukjah's Sketchfab sculpt: download disabled and a fixed figure pose; pulling it out of the viewer would take a model its author chose not to share.
- GigaBlenad's Sketchfab model (CC BY): downloadable, but unrigged and 3.75M faces.
- Chibifying a model by scaling its head: rejected, chibi proportions are drawn, not scaled.
- Subaru drawn by hand in SVG: rejected for a 3D render's accuracy.
